# Product Definition

ServiceFormAI OS is a DigiLocker-first, federated Citizen Service Intelligence Network where governments, municipalities, banks, universities, utilities, and private or public-private service providers publish schemes, services, benefits, renewals, and grievances once; citizens discover or receive relevant services through trusted channels; verified DigiLocker documents are reused with explicit consent; applications route to the correct federal, state, local, or private workflow; and citizens track every step until final resolution.

This is not a form builder.

It is the intelligence and execution layer for citizen services.

# Core UX Thesis

The product should move from:

“Find a form and fill 40 fields”

to:

“We found a relevant service. Your verified documents are ready. Consent, confirm missing details, submit, and track status.”

Design around these principles:

1. Trust before transaction
2. Consent before data sharing
3. Reuse before re-upload
4. Eligibility before long forms
5. Guidance before complexity
6. Status always visible
7. Citizen language over bureaucracy
8. Officers need speed and clarity
9. Admins need governance and audit
10. Local governments need templates, not complexity

---
